# 🌌 The 500 Year Plan For Humanity

**Year 2026 → Year 2526. Three objectives. Open to everyone. Owned by no one.**

---

## The Three Objectives

By the year 2526, humanity will have achieved the following:

### 01 · Humanity Beyond Earth
A billion people will call **Mars** home. Another billion will live on the **Moon and in artificial orbital habitats**. We become a multi-world species — not to abandon Earth, but to ensure no single catastrophe can end our story.

### 02 · A Life Worth Living, Fully
The average human lifespan reaches **300 years** — with the health, energy, and vitality of a 25-year-old for the entire duration. Not just longer life. *Better* life. Every single year of it.

### 03 · No One Left Behind
Every person on Earth — and beyond — receives **free air, water, food, shelter, education, medicine**, and all resources necessary to live with dignity. Not charity. A baseline. A floor beneath every human life.

---

## Why 500 Years?

500 years is only 5 generations. You → your children → their children → their children → their children. A baby born today could hold a grandchild who lives to see all three objectives met.

It's not so far. It just requires that we start *now* and never stop.

---

## This Plan Is Open Source

We know the destination. We don't pretend to know every step.

The milestones, strategies, and breakthroughs needed to get from 2026 to 2526 — those belong to scientists, engineers, farmers, teachers, artists, and dreamers who haven't been born yet.

**That's where you come in.**

## How To Contribute

This repository is the living document of how humanity gets there. There are no wrong answers — only unexplored paths.

### 🗺️ Propose a Milestone
Open an **Issue** with the label `milestone`. Include:
- The approximate year it should be achieved (e.g. *2075*)
- Which objective(s) it advances
- Why it's a necessary step
- Any relevant research, precedent, or inspiration

### 🔀 Propose a Strategy
Open a **Pull Request** modifying `MILESTONES.md` with a proposed pathway. Discussion happens in the PR. The community decides together.

### 💬 Debate & Refine
Use **Discussions** to challenge assumptions, propose alternatives, or raise problems with existing milestones. Productive disagreement is how this gets better.

### 📋 Contribution Guidelines
- The **three objectives are fixed**. Everything else is open.
- Be specific. "Achieve world peace" is not a milestone. "Establish a binding international framework for resource distribution covering 90% of nations by 2075" is.
- Think in systems, not just technology. Social, political, economic, and cultural milestones matter as much as scientific ones.
- Be kind. This spans centuries and continents. Humility is required.
