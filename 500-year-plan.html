Five centuries. Three promises. One species, finally living up to its potential. This was not written by a government. Not by a corporation. Not by any single person. It was started by a small group of humans who got tired of waiting — and handed to everyone else to finish.

The Three Objectives · To Be Achieved By 2526

01
Humanity Beyond Earth

A billion people will call Mars home. Another billion will live on the Moon and in artificial habitats among the stars. We become a multi-world species — not to abandon Earth, but to ensure no single catastrophe can end our story.

02
A Life Worth Living, Fully

The average human lifespan reaches 300 years — with the health, energy, and vitality of a 25-year-old for the entire duration. Not just longer life. Better life. Every single year of it.

03
No One Left Behind

Every person on Earth — and beyond — receives free air, water, food, shelter, education, medicine, and all resources necessary to live with dignity. Not charity. A baseline. A floor beneath every human life.

Open Source · The Path Is Yours To Write

We know where we're going. We don't pretend to know every step. That's the point. The milestones, the strategies, the breakthroughs needed to get there — those belong to scientists, engineers, farmers, teachers, artists, and dreamers who haven't been born yet.

This plan is open. Contribute a milestone. Challenge an assumption. Fork it entirely. The only rule: the three objectives stay. Everything else is up for debate.
